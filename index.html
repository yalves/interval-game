      How to play

      There's a new puzzle every day. Listen to two piano notes and name the interval between them in 4 guesses or fewer.

      An interval is the distance between two notes, measured in semitones (each neighboring key on the piano, white or black, is one semitone). The second note can be higher or lower than the first.

      Colors

      • Correct interval.
      • Wrong, but in the same family (perfect consonance, imperfect consonance or dissonance).
      • Wrong, and in a different family.

      Families

      • Perfect consonances: perfect 4th, perfect 5th, octave.
      • Imperfect consonances: 3rds and 6ths.
      • Dissonances: 2nds, 7ths and the tritone.

      Hints

      Each miss unlocks a new hint: first you can hear the notes together, then you learn whether the interval is small or large, and finally its family.
